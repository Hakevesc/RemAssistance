## Style Settings (Use for Every Prompt)

Append these settings (or their equivalent in Google Flow) to keep all clips visually consistent:

* **Length:** 10 seconds
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (or 9:16 if creating for Reels/Shorts)
* **Style:** Premium corporate commercial
* **Quality:** Photorealistic, cinematic, 4K
* **Camera:** Smooth dolly, slider, orbit, or gimbal movements only
* **Lighting:** Natural daylight with subtle blue/orange corporate accents
* **Depth of Field:** Shallow, cinematic
* **Frame Rate:** 24 fps
* **Mood:** Professional, optimistic, innovative
* **Negative Prompt:** No text, subtitles, watermarks, logos, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, low resolution, blurry visuals, cartoon style, exaggerated AI artifacts.

These prompts will produce a cohesive, high-end corporate video while clearly emphasizing Ethiopian talent in the Africa-focused scenes.
